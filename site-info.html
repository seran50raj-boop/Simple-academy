Learning · Privacy · Transparency

Important information.

SIMple Academy is an independently conceived and created educational project by Dr. Rajkumar Elanjeran, an Emergency Physician and Simulation Educator. It explores adult learning through visual examples, questions and reflection.

Educational disclaimer

Materials are provided for education and discussion. Any clinical examples are illustrative; they are not patient-specific medical advice, an approved treatment protocol, or a substitute for trained professional judgement, supervision or local clinical guidelines.

Accreditation: This rebuilt Foundations preview does not award accredited CME/CPD credit, issue a completion certificate or independently certify clinical competency.

Learner honour code

We ask learners to answer the activities themselves without using AI, answer generators or other outside assistance. Reviewing explanations and revisiting earlier cards is encouraged. This is a trust-based learning agreement, not a proctored examination.

Privacy and progress in this version

The current 14-card Andragogy course does not require a name, email address or account. It saves unlocked card progress, correctly answered questions and acceptance of the honour code in your browser's localStorage.

  • This course does not send those answers to a SIMple Academy learner database or sync them between devices.
  • Your progress may be lost if you clear site data, use a different browser, or browse in private mode.
  • To reset your local course history, clear this website's stored site data in your browser.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site; normal network requests may be handled or logged by GitHub or other infrastructure according to their own policies. This page does not govern hosting-provider logs.

Previous pilot: The older two-module experience used email sign-in and server-based progress features. Those are separate from this rebuilt course. Any personal data previously entered into the older pilot is not automatically removed or transferred by this website redesign.

Independence and content

SIMple Academy was independently conceived as an educational resource and is not represented here as an accredited medical training programme or as an affiliate of any other learning platform. Third-party concepts and theories remain attributed to their original authors where discussed in lessons.

For research, accredited education, official workplace assessment or clinical competence evaluation, arrange the appropriate institutional review and assessment process separately.

Available learning

This release contains four interactive modules: 14-card Foundations of Andragogy, 14-card Knowles’s Six Assumptions, 14-card Kolb’s Experiential Learning Cycle, and 14-card Rapid Cycle Deliberate Practice (RCDP). Each has six formative question checkpoints. Progress is stored locally in the learner’s browser; lessons do not award accredited CME/CPD credit or certify clinical competency. An earlier two-module pilot remains separately available as a historical preview.
