THE BIG IDEA

What is Andragogy?

A simple way to think about adult learning is to start with three things adults often bring into the room.

AAutonomyA say in how learning happens
EExperiencePrior knowledge worth using
PPurposeA reason to learn it now
YES OR NO?

Adults arrive as a blank slate.

Choose one.

THE CORE IDEA

Start with the adult learner.

Andragogy is an approach to understanding how adults learn. It draws attention to learner autonomy, experience and the need to see a purpose in learning.

YES OR NO?

Andragogy means adults should always teach themselves.

Choose one.

IMPORTANT NUANCE

Assumptions, not rules.

Andragogy describes useful assumptions about adult learners—not fixed rules that fit every adult in every situation.

YES OR NO?

Can an educator still set non-negotiable safety standards?

Choose one.

EXPLAIN · JOHN DEWEY

Not every experience is educative.

Dewey's concern is the quality and direction of experience—not merely whether something happened.

↗

Educative

An experience that supports growth and makes further learning possible.

↘

Mis-educative

An experience that arrests or distorts subsequent learning.

A nuance worth remembering

A debrief could include both reflection and conceptualisation, but they are not automatically the same thing. Learners need to move beyond recounting events to develop or revise an idea they can test.

We are exploring Kolb's experiential cycle here—not classifying learners into fixed learning-style types.
