# Simple Academy — Six anchor illustrations / production briefs
Updated: 2026-11-22 · Status: **art direction, not artwork approval**
Scope: Andragogy Foundations cards 1, 3, 5, 8, 12, 14.
User reference: uploaded illustration of four adult collaborators around a large diagonally oriented jigsaw document. Preserve its **warm vivid terracotta, coral, yellow, sky blue, cream and deep brown**, faceless adult figures, matte grain, simple organic silhouettes and soft shadows. The later approved iteration was **flatter and slightly more minimal**. Do **not** use the currently generated “clinician at desk / floating UI cards” imagery as a substitute for the collaborative puzzle.

## Shared art system — non-negotiable
- **Full-scene illustration, not enlarged navigation icon**; meaningful human/object relationships, at most one primary metaphor and 2–3 subsidiary props.
- **No faces**: no eyes, mouths, eyebrows or emotional expression. Human anatomy must remain coherent despite simplified shapes: unbroken neck/torso, visible shoulder and wrist joins, plausible elbow orientation, no malformed hand silhouettes and consistent posture/occlusion.
- Use 5–7 intentional colour regions per scene, with vivid flat fills (coral #F27850, terracotta #CF795A, mustard #F2AF4C, sky #9BCBD9, mint #EAF4EF, cream #FFF5E8, chocolate #654640). Add a subtle matte/noise texture at presentation resolution; soft contact shadows only.
- White/open background, generous negative space; no baked-in labels, progress UI, slide titles, or visible brand text in the artwork. The application renders those in accessible HTML. Do not generate a screenshot of a complete e-learning card when an illustration asset is requested.
- Work first at a **static 4:3 landscape illustration** (nominal 1200 × 900) that crops safely in 16:9 desktop column and 4:3 mobile. Key action in centre 70%; 8% safety margin. No clipped hands or feet.
- Exclude photographic realism, 3D gradients, outlines around all forms, luminous/glassy widgets, cartoon expressions, text-heavy posters and a stethoscope as a generic stand-in for andragogy.
- File names: `card-01-andragogy`, `card-03-learner`, `card-05-assumptions`, `card-08-history`, `card-12-simulation`, `card-14-remember`. Final editable layered SVG expected for purposeful animation; if source is a raster image, present it as a *static reference* until vector layers are built and verified.

## 01 — ANDRAGOGY / “Learning fits together”
**Core message**: Experience, autonomy and purpose co-create meaningful adult learning.

**Exact composition**:
- One generous, diagonal, cream-coloured jigsaw document dominates the centre, roughly 60% of scene.
- Four **faceless adult collaborators** around it, in simple, plausible actions: LEFT coral-shirted adult standing and leaning with palm resting on a table edge; TOP sky-blue-shirted adult seated behind the table holding an independent puzzle piece; RIGHT terracotta-clad adult standing with hands at sides or one hand resting gently on table; LOWER mustard-clad adult seated at near corner pointing with one clearly attached forearm.
- Arrange adult silhouettes and hands so no figure appears kneeling on the board or crawling across the paper. The tabletop may hide pelvis and legs, but all visible limbs must be attached.
- Puzzle has 3 muted shape lines and **one mustard piece in transit**. No labels on pieces. Avoid unrelated computers, floating chat panels or medical equipment.

**Anatomy gate**: minimum 4 adult torsos/heads appear connected, each visible arm flows from a visible shoulder, hand reaches its intended surface, no reversed elbow/wrist; read each silhouette at thumbnail scale.

**Motion after static approval**: move **only the independent mustard piece** along a short path into position; optional subtle hand follow-through if there is a genuinely separate SVG hand/forearm group with a correct elbow. Do not animate whole bodies for decoration.

**Alternative if four people compromise anatomy**: three adults standing around the same board with one partial torso occluded. Never add a fourth malformed body just to match the count.

## 03 — LEARNER / “Start with the adult learner”
**Core message**: Adult learners have an active voice in route, resources and pacing.

**Exact composition**:
- A faceless adult at the centre-left of a large angled cream paper map; torso visible, shoulders naturally placed, one hand hovering over one of two genuine paths.
- Three resource stations shown as different objects (practice kit, notes, peer dialogue) in coral/mustard/blue along the map. They are objects, not floating app notifications.
- Path is curved and visual, not a generic coloured infographic line on white.

**Anatomy gate**: one person only; do not expose lower legs; pointing arm emerges from shoulder with readable elbow.

**Motion**: a single learner marker travels from the start to the selected station, pause and settle; optional destination soft halo.

## 05 — ASSUMPTIONS / “Assumptions, not rules”
**Core message**: Adult-learning assumptions are helpful interpretive lenses rather than rigid laws.

**Exact composition**:
- Three oversized, **physical transparent colour frames** arranged on a cream tabletop, held up by stands or layered over a single teaching scene.
- The same tiny shared scene (book + practice object + learner-choice path) is seen differently through each coral, mustard and blue lens.
- Use a rich editorial still life with clear light/shadow and purposeful overlaps; **not** merely a Venn diagram.

**Anatomy gate**: no human bodies needed. Ensure each frame has plausible material layering.

**Motion**: translate one frame slowly over the central scene, highlighting the change in interpretation without zoom or bounce.

## 08 — HISTORY / “Kapp to Knowles”
**Core message**: The term predates the modern model; the modern model was developed/popularized later.

**Exact composition**:
- A wide desk with an older open book, a midway paper ribbon, and a modern minimalist teaching board connected by a single bent timeline.
- Historical stages as tangible objects in mustard, terracotta and sky blue; the objects imply passage of time and change in learning ideas.
- Avoid portraits of historical figures if anatomical or factual resemblance is uncertain.
- The app supplies `1833`, `Kapp`, `Knowles` as external HTML labels (not inside asset).

**Anatomy gate**: no humans.

**Motion**: a page gently turns, then a short chronological line draws from older book to contemporary board. Respect reduced-motion preference.

## 12 — SIMULATION / “Structure and choice coexist”
**Core message**: The safety standard can stay fixed while adult learners choose how and when to practise.

**Exact composition**:
- One coherent clinical simulation bay: correct-scale stretcher with pillow, clearly labelled-in-HTML simulation equipment cluster, display with a **non-diagnostic abstract trace**, and two route cards or practice alternatives on a real trolley surface.
- Show an optional **simple faceless facilitator and resident** standing behind equipment with anatomy that reads clearly; do not put a clinician’s arm through the bed or invent additional limbs. If figures cannot meet the anatomy gate, show the bay without humans rather than distorted ones.
- Keep monitor/simulator anatomy credible: stable connected tubing where shown, correctly positioned bed/head-end, grounded monitor/support. Avoid pseudo-medical apparatus.

**Anatomy gate**: independent review of each visible body joint plus medical object relationships. No invented clinical monitor values or inaccurate waveforms.

**Motion**: 1.5-second trace sweep on monitor and highlight one of two practice-route choices. Never simulate a misleading rhythm or safety cue.

## 14 — REMEMBER / “Three ideas to carry forward”
**Core message**: Learner, experience, and meaningful choice within clear standards.

**Exact composition**:
- Three tangible dimensional-but-flat editorial objects placed on a single table: experience portfolio, learner route tile and a safety-checked practice kit. Objects converge to form one memorable centre composition.
- One warm-shadowed cream backdrop, 2–3 small accents in coral, yellow and blue, plenty of white space.
- Avoid a giant three-colour checkmark diagram; communicate consolidation by a real arrangement of learning artefacts.

**Anatomy gate**: no humans needed.

**Motion**: each of the three objects slides a few pixels into a well-fitting position, with each phase retained visibly.

## Acceptance procedure (all six)
1. Render **the actual artwork itself**; do not judge only SVG source or generator output description.
2. Visually inspect full-size and thumbnails on **desktop 1320px and mobile 390px**. The same composition must fit without cropped hands, distorted scaling or unreadable narrative.
3. For humans, scrutinize neck-to-shoulder, shoulder-to-upper-arm, elbow-to-forearm, wrist-to-hand, hips/legs if visible, object contact, silhouette balance and implausible overlaps. Reject on the first structural failure.
4. Verify vibrant fills remain saturated and subtle matte texture remains perceptible but not muddy on white.
5. Only after user visual sign-off: convert/retain independently editable layers, implement reduced-motion-safe purposeful animation, then run actual Chromium screenshots and interaction tests.
6. Do not swap placeholder assets in production or merge the draft PR without explicit review.
